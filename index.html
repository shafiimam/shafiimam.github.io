The story

I loved commerce
before it was my job.

I don't treat your store like a ticket. I treat it like mine — because I've been on your side of it.

  1. 2020

    It started with a degree — and an obsession.

    I finished my CS degree with one thing pulling at me harder than the rest: e-commerce. The mix of technology and the way people buy. For my capstone, I built a complete, custom e-commerce system on a mid-to-large-scale architecture — not a toy, a real one.

  2. 2021

    Then I ran my own store.

    I started my own e-commerce business. I felt every part of it — the late-night fixes, the sale that doesn't come, the small change that suddenly works. That's when it stopped being theory. I understood what merchants actually feel, because I was one.

  3. Then

    I went all-in on Shopify.

    I joined a Shopify company and went deep as a full-stack developer — themes, apps, the whole ecosystem, living in the docs. Shopify became the craft I chose to master, because it's where the brands I care about actually live.

  4. Now

    200+ merchants, 6+ years, still obsessed.

    Theme development, app integrations, custom apps, customer flows, AI integration — for more than 200 Shopify merchants and counting. Every store I touch, I want it faster, smarter, and selling more than the day I found it. That part never got old.
